Michael from the YouTube channel the Iron Snail talks about Quince, a [tech company that makes clothes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HFjJAm6AuiM).

Undercutting high end fashion brands by using manufacturer to customer sales and margin tradeoffs on their clothing. Where the clothing secretly isn't even the product, the tech is.


> They want to sell their technology and their know-how to other brands because Quince is a technology company before anything else.
> And if, say, 10 giant brands do want in, all of a sudden, Quince may not need to sell anything to keep the lights on.
